# 8. Level-up and title ceremony: system popup

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Source:** [Handoff 0001](../handoffs/0001-progression-system.md) (open question, answered by Wynand)

## Context
The handoff asked what the level-up and title-unlock moments should look like.

## Decision
The ceremony is a full-screen Solo Leveling "system window": a dark overlay with a glowing cyan panel. It shows **LEVEL UP!**, the level change, the stat, skill point and fatigue gains, and a **TITLE ACQUIRED** section when a new title is unlocked. Tapping dismisses it. When several level-ups happen at once they queue, and they appear after the daily completion animation.

## Consequences
The animation (fade, scale and glow) is a placeholder until final assets exist. The Goku transformation animation is a separate feature, and its trigger is still undecided.
